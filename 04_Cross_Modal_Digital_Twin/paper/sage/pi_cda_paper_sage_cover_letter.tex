Dear Editor,

\vspace{0.5em}

We submit the manuscript ``Digital-Twin Pretraining for Label-Efficient
Skeleton Action Recognition in Industrial Workcells'' for consideration as an
Original Research Article in the International Journal of Advanced Robotic
Systems.

The manuscript studies virtual-to-physical transfer for skeleton action
recognition in industrial workcells. It evaluates unsupervised adaptation and
label-efficient adaptation under subject-disjoint testing, and shows how
digital-twin pretraining can reduce the amount of physical annotation required
for target-domain recognition. The topic fits the journal's scope in robotics,
human--robot collaboration, and intelligent sensing.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the
manuscript and consent to its submission. The submission includes a
double-anonymized manuscript, a separate Title Page, the cover letter, and the
individual figure files.

The authors declare no potential conflicts of interest. The funding and author-contribution statements are supplied on the Title
Page.
