We are pleased to submit our original manuscript entitled "Danger-State Detection in Human-Robot Collaboration Using Deep Learning" by Ameur Gargouri, Mohamed Karray, and Mohamed Ksantini for consideration as a Letter in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L).

In physical Human-Robot Collaboration (pHRC), ensuring safety under standards like ISO/TS 15066 while maintaining industrial productivity is a major challenge due to the false-alarm dilemma: overly sensitive systems trigger unnecessary stops, while missed hazards risk worker injury. Rather than relying on expensive force/torque sensors, model-dependent observers, or occlusion-prone cameras, this letter presents an end-to-end deep learning framework operating on high-frequency wearable IMU telemetry.

Key contributions of this letter include:
1. GPU-Accelerated Kinematic Expansion: Deriving velocity and acceleration on the fly from 65-channel IMU position streams, expanding them into 195 rich physical features without extra hardware or storage overhead.
2. Systematic 1D Architecture Benchmark: A standardized comparison of five 1D architectures (ConvNeXt 1D, TCN, MLP-Mixer 1D, Transformer 1D, and InceptionTime) with test-time augmentation. ConvNeXt 1D and TCN achieve macro-F1 scores above 98.6% with >98% precision and >97% recall.
3. Dedicated False-Alarm Mitigation: A temporal debouncing stage (3-tap median filter and threshold optimization) that slashes false positives by 87.5% while preserving >97.1% danger recall, establishing a viable operating point for real-world cobot deployment.
4. Extensive Multi-Subject Validation: Statistically validated across 60 human subjects (>69,000 temporal windows) on the DASIG industrial benchmark.

This work directly aligns with RA-L's scope in safe human-robot interaction, intelligent cobot perception, and applied machine learning in robotics. 

This manuscript represents original work, has not been published previously, and is not currently under consideration elsewhere. All authors have reviewed and approved this submission.
